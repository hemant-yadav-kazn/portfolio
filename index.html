(01) — About the work

Precision in
Motion.

I run hospitality operations the way a race engineer runs a pit stop: every motion accounted for, every second measured, nothing left to improvisation. Formula 1 doesn't win races on raw speed alone — it wins on the precision of a two-second tire change, repeated flawlessly under pressure, event after event. That is the standard I hold my own operations to.

Whether I am coordinating a member event at Lambton Golf & Country Club, running the floor at the Royal Canadian Yacht Club, or building a digital campaign for KAZN, my approach doesn't change: eliminate friction before it happens, build systems that hold up under volume, and treat consistency as the real competitive advantage — not a single good night, but twenty-four of them in a row. I'm currently open to full-time opportunities in Toronto — if that overlap sounds useful to you, let's talk.
